How ranking works

Comment karma is not public, so each course is scored from who recommended it, how strongly, in how many separate discussions, and for how long.

  1. Stance. Every comment is labelled for the course it mentions: strongly recommends (1.0), recommends (0.7), mentions (0.25) or criticizes (0).
  2. One vote per person. Someone who recommends a course five times counts once, at their strongest stance.
  3. Pile-ons are damped. Within one thread, votes decay by half each (1, ½, ¼…). Ten replies in one thread are worth less than two independent threads; the same course surfacing in ten unrelated threads counts ten times.
  4. Staying power. Each extra calendar year in which people recommended it adds 8%.

Trending uses the same votes, halving their weight every two years back from the newest comment in the data.

Badges

Classic
Top-ranked and recommended across at least eight years.
Evergreen
Recommended in six or more different years.
Rising
Most of its support came in the last three years of data.
Hidden gem
Only two to four recommenders, but nearly all of them emphatic.

Comments were found with keyword rules and reviewed by AI models (Claude Haiku 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5), which also merged course name variants, labelled stances and wrote the summaries. Expect a few misses.
