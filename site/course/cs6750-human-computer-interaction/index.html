CS6750 Human-Computer Interaction

Georgia Tech

CS6750 is a Georgia Tech graduate-level course in human-computer interaction, covering the theory and methods behind designing and evaluating user interfaces. Its lectures are freely available and it is also offered as part of the OMSCS program.

Commenters praise it for teaching something new with interesting underlying theory, and note that its lectures are free.
