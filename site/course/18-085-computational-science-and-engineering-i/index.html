18.085 Computational Science and Engineering I

MIT OpenCourseWare

3 recommenders · 3 threads · recommended in 3 years, 2011–2025 · score 3.1

Go to course ↗

MIT's 18.085 Computational Science and Engineering I, taught by Gilbert Strang, covers applied linear algebra, differential equations, and solving models numerically, often in MATLAB. The course is built around the companion text 'Computational Science and Engineering' and its recorded lectures.

Commenters call it one of the best math courses they've taken and praise Strang as a fantastic teacher, with lectures available free on MIT OpenCourseWare.
