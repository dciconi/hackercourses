Crash Course World History is a YouTube video series by John Green that surveys human history from early civilizations through the modern era in short, animated lectures. It is one of several Crash Course playlists covering many subjects.

Commenters describe it as highly recommended for learning history in an entertaining way and say its content is generally good.
