Math 55

Harvard University

1 recommender · 1 thread · recommended in 1 year, 2014 · score 0.7

Search for it ↗

Math 55 is Harvard's famously intensive undergraduate course in abstract and proof-based mathematics, covering topics like group theory, linear algebra, and analysis at a rigorous, theory-first pace. It is known for demanding problem sets and a heavy workload.

Commenters call it an incredible class, pointing to it as a standout example of Harvard's theoretical math training.
