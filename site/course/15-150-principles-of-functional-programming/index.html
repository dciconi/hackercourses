15-150 Principles of Functional Programming

Carnegie Mellon University

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2014–2023 · score 1.8

Search for it ↗

An introductory Carnegie Mellon University course on functional programming, taught in Standard ML, covering recursion, types, and reasoning about programs without mutable state. It is part of CMU's undergraduate curriculum and was co-designed by Bob Harper.

Commenters describe it as a course where each homework made them feel they had unlocked a new level of reasoning with code, and as one that discourages operational reasoning in favor of ML/Haskell-style thinking.
