CS186 is UC Berkeley's introductory database systems course, taught by Joseph Hellerstein, covering topics such as storage, buffer management, query processing and transactions. Commenters point to its lecture videos.

Commenters suggest the lectures are worth watching in full, and one singles out a lecture on caching strategies as a good basis for understanding cache tradeoffs.
