CS25: Transformers United

Stanford University

CS25 is a Stanford University seminar-style course on Transformer models, the architecture behind many modern large language and vision models. It covers the core ideas and recent research applications of Transformers.

One commenter calls the Stanford courses, including the Transformer one, a safe bet among the many university lectures available online.
