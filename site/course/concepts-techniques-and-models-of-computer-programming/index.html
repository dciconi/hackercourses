Concepts, Techniques, and Models of Computer Programming

UCLouvain

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2014–2019 · score 1.7

Go to course ↗

A university textbook and companion edX MOOC by Peter Van Roy (UCLouvain) that teaches programming through the Oz language, covering multiple paradigms (functional, imperative, OOP, logic, concurrent) in a single language. The edX course is commenters' description of a free introduction covering the first three chapters.

Commenters call it a good resource for self-study that covers multiple paradigms in one language, and one says it helped bridge the gap from casual scripting to understanding how loops and recursion work.
