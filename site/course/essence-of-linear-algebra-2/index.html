Essence of Linear Algebra

3Blue1Brown

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2019–2020 · score 1.6

Search for it ↗

A visual, animation-driven video series by Grant Sanderson that builds geometric intuition for vectors, linear transformations, matrices, determinants, and eigenvectors. It is meant as an intuition companion rather than a full problem-solving course.

Commenters describe it as a great way to build geometric intuition for linear algebra, with one noting its videos on stats are very digestible.
