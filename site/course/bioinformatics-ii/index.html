Bioinformatics II is the second part of Coursera's bioinformatics course, covering algorithms applied to biological problems with a textbook and automatically graded programming problems. It was offered as a free online course with scheduled sessions.

Commenters describe it as one of the most enjoyable Coursera courses they've taken, praising how it applies algorithms to a non-CS field.
