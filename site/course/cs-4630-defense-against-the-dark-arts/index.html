CS 4630 Defense Against the Dark Arts is a University of Virginia course on software security, covering low-level attacks such as return-oriented programming and the defenses against them. The course materials are openly available online.

One commenter calls it an awesome, seemingly open courseware class they found while learning about return-oriented programming.
