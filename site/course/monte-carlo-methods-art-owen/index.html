Monte Carlo Methods (Art Owen)

Stanford University

A Stanford course by Art Owen covering Monte Carlo methods for simulation and estimation, including variance reduction and Markov chain Monte Carlo techniques. The course materials are published on the instructor's Stanford site with a syllabus.

One commenter calls it a great course on Monte Carlo methods and points to its syllabus while arguing the field is rich and hard.
