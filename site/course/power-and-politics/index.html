Power and Politics

Yale University

Power and Politics is a Yale University introductory political science course taught by Ian Shapiro that surveys global politics and how the modern world came to be. It is delivered as lecture videos.

One commenter calls it an excellent zoom-out into global politics and how the modern world was made.
