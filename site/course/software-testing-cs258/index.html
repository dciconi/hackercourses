Software Testing (CS258)

Udacity

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2015–2017 · score 1.5

Go to course ↗

A free Udacity course taught by Andreas Zeller (the author of the delta debugging work) that introduces software testing techniques, including automated test generation and fuzzing. It is one of the course's topics covered alongside a companion debugging course.

Commenters point to it as a well-regarded free course for learning software testing, with one noting it is taught by the author of csmith.
