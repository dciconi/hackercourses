CS244 Advanced Topics in Networking

Stanford University

1 recommender · 1 thread · recommended in 1 year, 2012 · score 1.0

Search for it ↗

CS244 is a Stanford graduate-level networking course taught in a seminar and reading-heavy style, covering advanced topics in computer networks. Per the commenter, it was once a project-heavy course where students built large parts of a network stack, and later became more of a literature review course.

One commenter calls it probably their favorite class at Stanford, recalling that under McKeown it had students build essentially the entire internet over the quarter.
