The Nature of Code

YouTube

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2019–2020 · score 1.7

Go to course ↗

The Nature of Code is Daniel Shiffman's free video series from The Coding Train on simulating natural systems with code. It covers topics such as vectors, forces, oscillation, particle systems, autonomous agents, and genetic algorithms, with the math and algorithms explained alongside the code.

Commenters describe it as a fantastic explanation of the algorithms and math behind simulations and AI, and one who followed it used it to build 2D canvas simulations even though the examples are not in Python.
