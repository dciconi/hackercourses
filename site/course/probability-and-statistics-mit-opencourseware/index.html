Probability and Statistics (MIT OpenCourseWare)

MIT OCW

MIT OpenCourseWare's probability and statistics course, delivered as a series of video lectures from MIT. It covers probability theory and statistical inference foundations used in machine learning and data science.

Commenters call the MIT lectures very in-depth and concise, and recommend them as foundational knowledge for machine learning work.
