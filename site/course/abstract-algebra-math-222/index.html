Abstract Algebra (Math 222)

Harvard Extension School

3 recommenders · 3 threads · recommended in 3 years, 2011–2016 · score 3.1

Go to course ↗

A Harvard Extension School abstract algebra course taught by Benedict Gross, with recorded lectures covering the standard undergraduate abstract algebra material (groups, rings, fields). The lectures are available online, including a YouTube playlist from a Fall 2003 offering.

Commenters call Benedict Gross a great lecturer and describe the course as a really great abstract algebra course for pure math learners.
