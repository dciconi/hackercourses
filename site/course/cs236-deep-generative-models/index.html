CS236: Deep Generative Models

Stanford University

1 recommender · 2 threads · recommended in 1 year, 2025 · score 1.0

Stanford's CS236 Deep Generative Models course, taught by Stefano Ermon, covers modern deep generative models such as likelihood-based models, latent variable models, and generative adversarial networks. Its full lecture series is available free on YouTube.

Commenters call Stefano Ermon's lectures amazing and point to the full YouTube series as a video option for learning generative AI.
