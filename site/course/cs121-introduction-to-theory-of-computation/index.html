CS121: Introduction to Theory of Computation

Harvard University

1 recommender · 1 thread · recommended in 1 year, 2020 · score 0.7

An introductory Harvard course on the theory of computation, covering formal models of computing such as automata and formal languages, along with computability and complexity. It is taught by Boaz Barak, based on the course site linked in the comment.

The commenter lists it among the courses they 'definitely recommend' for a CS-oriented online degree, alongside CS50 and CS124.
