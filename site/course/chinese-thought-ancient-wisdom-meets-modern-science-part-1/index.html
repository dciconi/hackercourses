A UBCx course on edX covering early Chinese philosophy and religious thought, including Shang and Zhou religion, the Analects of Confucius, the Daodejing, Mozi, and the Guodian texts. Part 1 introduces the basic philosophical, religious, and scientific concepts used throughout the course.
