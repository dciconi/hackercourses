The Art of Doing Science and Engineering (Richard Hamming)

YouTube

3 recommenders · 3 threads · recommended in 3 years, 2017–2023 · score 3.5

Go to course ↗

A video lecture series by Richard Hamming, based on a graduate course he taught at the U.S. Naval Postgraduate School in 1995, covering how to do research and learn hard subjects. The lectures are accompanied by a book with the same title.

Commenters call it an incredible, pure-gold course on how to learn and do science, and recommend the lectures alongside the book.
