An undergraduate operating systems course taught by Paul Krzyzanowski at Rutgers University, covering core OS concepts such as processes, scheduling, memory, and file systems. Course materials and syllabus are published on the instructor's course site.

Commenters describe it as a quick, concise, pragmatic way to learn operating systems.
