A short beginner Blender course by Grant Abbitt on Udemy built around modeling a donut, introducing the core 3D modeling workflow in Blender. It serves as a hands-on first project before his longer Blender courses.

A commenter calls Grant Abbitt's Udemy Blender courses excellent and says the course's discounted price bought 14 hours of material from basics through UVs, texture painting, rigging and animation.
