Beyond the Goal (Eliyahu Goldratt)

A lecture series by Eliyahu Goldratt, the physicist behind the Theory of Constraints, that goes beyond the ideas of his novel The Goal and covers the broader principles of his thinking about production, throughput, and constraints.

One commenter strongly suggests these lectures over The Goal, saying the novel is thin on useful information by comparison.
