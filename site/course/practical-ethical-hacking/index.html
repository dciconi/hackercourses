Practical Ethical Hacking

Udemy

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2020–2021 · score 2.1

Search for it ↗

A Udemy course by Heath Adams (The Cyber Mentor) that walks learners through ethical hacking and penetration testing, with hands-on work on machines from Hack The Box and TryHackMe. Commenters describe it as a practical, boot-camp-like introduction to pentesting.

Commenters call it about as close to an in-person cyber boot camp as you can get and say it is cheap for the good material it provides.
