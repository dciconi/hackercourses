Moral Philosophy (PHIL 181)

Yale University

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 1 year, 2025 · score 1.4

Go to course ↗

Yale's introductory moral philosophy course (PHIL 181), taught as video lectures through Open Yale Courses, surveys how philosophers have answered questions about living well and flourishing. Lectures cover topics such as attachment, detachment, and utility-based accounts of the good life.

Commenters recommend it as a useful survey of how philosophers have addressed meaning and flourishing, with one pointing to the lecture on detachment and another urging viewers to watch the whole series.
