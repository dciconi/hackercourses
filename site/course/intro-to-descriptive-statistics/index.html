An introductory Udacity course on descriptive statistics, covering basic ways to summarize and describe data such as measures of center, spread, and distributions. It is one of two free introductory statistics courses Udacity offers before its AI nanodegree.

Commenters point to it as a free starting point for statistics, one of the introductory courses Udacity recommends before its AI nanodegree.
