Plasma Physics and Fusion

edX

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2018–2021 · score 1.5

Search for it ↗

An edX course covering the physics of plasmas and the engineering approaches to fusion energy, including magnetic confinement devices such as tokamaks. Commenters mention it as a source on why net-energy fusion on Earth is hard.

Commenters point to it as one of edX's distinctive courses not available elsewhere, and one suggests it gives useful context on the feasibility of fusion reactors.
