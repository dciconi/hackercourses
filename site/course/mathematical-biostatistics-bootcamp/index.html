Mathematical Biostatistics Bootcamp

Coursera

1 recommender · 1 thread · recommended in 1 year, 2015 · score 0.7

A Coursera bootcamp from Johns Hopkins covering the mathematical foundations of biostatistics, presented as a quick but careful treatment of the math behind statistical methods. Commenters contrast it with the MITx probability course and the Coursera data science specialization.

One commenter says it is a little more careful about the math than the courses in the data science specialization, though it is quicker than the MITx probability course.
