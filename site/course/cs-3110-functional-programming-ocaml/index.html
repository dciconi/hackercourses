CS 3110 Functional Programming (OCaml)

Cornell University

3 recommenders · 3 threads · recommended in 3 years, 2014–2021 · score 2.8

Go to course ↗

CS 3110 is Cornell University's functional programming course taught with OCaml, covering functional programming concepts and the language. Its lecture notes and textbook are published online.

Commenters point to it as a good primer for learning OCaml and call its lecture notes high quality and well revised over the years.
