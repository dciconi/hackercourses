The Analytics Edge

edX

5 recommenders · 5 threads · recommended in 3 years, 2014–2017 · score 5.5

An edX MITx course (15.071x) introducing analytics and machine learning techniques, such as regression, trees, and clustering, applied to real datasets using R. It emphasizes hands-on exercises and examples.

Commenters call it a great MOOC and the best analytics course they've taken, praising its hands-on exercises and above-average MITx quality.
