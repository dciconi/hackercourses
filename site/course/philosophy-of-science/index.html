    I can make one negative suggestion: while I did enjoy listening to Rob Ingles' narration of The Lord of the Rings, the parts about Frodo and Sam trudging through Mordor didn't exactly lift my spirits during August marathon training runs.

    Basically I just listened to whatever my library had. I did enjoy several classes from The Great Courses, which are now available as downloadable content.
