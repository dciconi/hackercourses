Technology Entrepreneurship

Stanford University

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2013–2016 · score 2.0

A Stanford University MOOC on technology entrepreneurship covering how to start and build technology ventures, with video lectures and an optional team project component. Commenters note the lectures are free while the course book is not.

Commenters point to it as a free alternative to paid pre-incubators, noting the video lectures are free and teams can be formed for hands-on projects.
