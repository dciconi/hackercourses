Introduction to Mathematical Thinking

Coursera

5 recommenders · 6 threads · recommended in 5 years, 2013–2021 · score 5.2

A Coursera course by Keith Devlin (Stanford) introducing mathematical language and reasoning, covering the habits of mathematical thought as a foundation for later math study. Commenters describe it as challenging but manageable.

Commenters call it a fantastic introduction to mathematical thinking and a good starter for real analysis, with one calling it one of their favorite MOOCs.
