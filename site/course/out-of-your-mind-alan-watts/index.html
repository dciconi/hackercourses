Out of Your Mind (Alan Watts)

Alan Watts

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2016–2017 · score 1.8

A set of recorded lectures by philosopher Alan Watts, in which he discusses consciousness, perception, and how ordinary habits of thought shape our experience of the world.

Commenters describe the lectures as amazing and highly recommend them, while noting Watts's books are wordier and less engaging than the spoken talks.
