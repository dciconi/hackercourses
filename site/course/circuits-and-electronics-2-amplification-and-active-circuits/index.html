The second course in MIT's 6.002x Circuits and Electronics sequence on edX, covering amplifiers, transistors, and active circuit design after the basics of passive circuits. It is part of the series that commenters point to as a route into electronics.

Commenters point to it as part of the MIT sequence on edX for learning electronics on your own, noting it is taught by the edX CEO and is the flagship course.
