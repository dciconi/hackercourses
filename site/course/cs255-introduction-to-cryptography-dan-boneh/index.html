CS255 Introduction to Cryptography (Dan Boneh)

Stanford University

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2015–2022 · score 2.3

Go to course ↗

Stanford's graduate-level introduction to modern cryptography taught by Dan Boneh, covering provable security and the design of cryptographic primitives and protocols. The course materials are published openly on the course site, and a Coursera version of the course also exists.

Commenters call Boneh's classes highly recommended and praise him for sharing the course materials openly, and one says the Coursera version is of similarly high quality with less depth.
