2.008x Manufacturing Processes and Systems

edX

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2022–2024 · score 1.8

Go to course ↗

An MIT edX course giving an overview of manufacturing processes and systems, covering methods such as casting, injection molding, and 3D printing. Commenters describe it as roughly 14 weeks at 4-6 hours per week if the homework is done fully.

Commenters call it a great overview of all types of manufacturing and a good place to start for learning the field.
