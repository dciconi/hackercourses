A Brief History of Humankind

Coursera

3 recommenders · 4 threads · recommended in 4 years, 2013–2023 · score 3.0

Go to course ↗

A Brief History of Humankind is a Coursera course taught by historian Yuval Harari that surveys the long history of Homo sapiens, from early human societies through the agricultural, scientific and modern eras. It was the basis for his book Sapiens.

Commenters describe it as a fun, enriching course, with one calling it the best enrichment course they had taken.
