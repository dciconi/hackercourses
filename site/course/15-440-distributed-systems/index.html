Carnegie Mellon University

An undergraduate introductory distributed systems course at Carnegie Mellon covering core topics such as communication, consistency, replication, and fault tolerance in distributed settings. Public lecture notes from several semesters are available online.

Commenters point to the course's lecture notes as a solid grounding in distributed systems, one saying it gave them a really good foundation and another calling it a great course.
