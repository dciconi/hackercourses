Django for Everybody

Coursera

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2020–2022 · score 1.8

Search for it ↗

A Django web development course by Charles Severance (Dr. Chuck) that covers building web applications with Django and the surrounding basics of HTML, CSS, SQL, and JavaScript. It is offered on Coursera as part of a Django specialization and also free at dj4e.com.

Commenters describe it as approachable and well rounded, and praise Severance's teaching style compared with the official Django tutorial.
