A Udacity course introducing the software and techniques behind self-driving cars, taught by Sebastian Thrun, who led Stanford's autonomous vehicle work. It is offered free and covers the computer science behind autonomous driving.

Commenters describe it as a great starting point for autonomous cars and a world-class computer-science education available for free from a leading AI expert.
