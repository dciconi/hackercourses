Intro to Programming

Lynda.com

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2015–2016 · score 1.5

Search for it ↗

A short introductory course on Lynda.com, taught mostly in JavaScript, that covers the logical basics of programming that carry over to any language. It is aimed at total beginners.

Commenters recommend it as an accessible starting point for absolute beginners, praising instructor Simon Allardice's clear explanations.
