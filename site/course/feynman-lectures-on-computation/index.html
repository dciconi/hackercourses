Feynman Lectures on Computation

Caltech

3 recommenders · 3 threads · recommended in 3 years, 2015–2026 · score 3.3

Search for it ↗

Richard Feynman's lecture notes on the theory of computation, covering how computers work at the level of physics and logic, including limits on computing such as the energy and entropy required per operation. Published as a book from lectures given at Caltech.

Commenters recommend it for its still-relevant treatment of computing and its habit of tying computation back to physics.
