Stanford neurobiologist Robert Sapolsky's lecture course on behavioral evolution, delivered as a recorded university course and posted on YouTube, covering how evolutionary thinking explains social behavior, cooperation, and game-theoretic strategies such as tit for tat.

Commenters say the lectures are worth watching in full and recommend them for the evolution of cooperation and related subjects.
