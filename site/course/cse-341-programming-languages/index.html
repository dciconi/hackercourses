CSE 341 Programming Languages

University of Washington

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2017–2023 · score 1.9

Go to course ↗

CSE 341 Programming Languages is the University of Washington undergraduate course taught by Dan Grossman, covering the design and semantics of programming languages, with a substantial functional-programming component in ML and Racket. The Coursera version of the course is a MOOC built on the same material.

Commenters call it an excellent undergraduate-level introduction to programming languages and recommend the recorded lectures, with one saying they could not stop watching the videos.
