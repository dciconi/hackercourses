Essence of Linear Algebra and Essence of Calculus

3Blue1Brown

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2020–2022 · score 1.9

Go to course ↗

A series of short animated video lectures by Grant Sanderson (3Blue1Brown) that builds geometric intuition for linear algebra (vectors, linear transformations, determinants, eigenvectors) and for calculus (derivatives, integrals, the fundamental theorem).

Commenters recommend it as a visual, intuition-first complement to more academic courses, useful for building a solid understanding of linear algebra and calculus before ML work.
