NYUx Cybersecurity Fundamentals

NYUx Cybersecurity Fundamentals is an edX course offered by NYU as part of its microBachelors cybersecurity track. It introduces core cybersecurity concepts and can be taken free per class, with a paid certificate option.

One commenter says cybersecurity pays well and calls the NYU course a "screaming deal" at $1,344 for the certificate, while noting each class can be taken free without certification.
