Design Thinking Bootcamp

Stanford University

1 recommender · 1 thread · recommended in 1 year, 2014 · score 0.7

Search for it ↗

A design thinking workshop run by Stanford's d.school (Hasso Plattner Institute of Design) that teaches a human-centered, prototype-driven process for solving problems. The commenter describes a short open in-person workshop, a PDF summary of the bootcamp materials, and a virtual crash course.

One commenter recommends it as a hands-on introduction to design thinking, especially useful for startups, and praises the quality of student work from the fuller Stanford courses.
