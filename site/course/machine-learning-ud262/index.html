Machine Learning (ud262)

Udacity

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 1 year, 2016 · score 1.4

Go to course ↗

Udacity's Machine Learning (ud262) is a free online course covering core machine learning concepts and techniques, with prerequisites in statistics and related math that the course links to. It is aimed at learners who want a general, approachable introduction before the heavier theory.

Commenters suggest it as an accessible MOOC for getting a general idea of machine learning without needing much math background first.
