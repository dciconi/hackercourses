Origins: Astronomy, Geology, Life, and the Universe

Coursera

1 recommender · 1 thread · recommended in 1 year, 2015 · score 1.0

Go to course ↗

An introductory Coursera course, taught as one theme per week, that traces the story of the universe from the formation of the solar system to the present day. It introduces the foundations and terminology of astronomy, geology, microbiology, paleontology, botany, and ecology.

A commenter loved it for covering a wide range of science disciplines in a chronological, one-theme-per-week structure.
