CS154 is Stanford's introductory course on automata and complexity theory, covering formal languages, finite automata, Turing machines, and computability and complexity. It is offered through the Stanford CS department's course site.

Commenters suggest it as a way to formalize abstract concepts and build abstract thinking, recommending it over informal anecdotes.
