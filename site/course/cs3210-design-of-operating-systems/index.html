CS3210 Design of Operating Systems is a Georgia Tech course built around writing an operating system kernel, with labs that have students implement OS components in Rust. Commenters point to its lab assignments as the core of the course.

Commenters describe it as one of their favorite undergrad courses, valuing how much general OS concepts they learned despite the frustration of the labs.
