CS 182 Designing, Visualizing and Understanding Deep Neural Networks

UC Berkeley

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2022–2023 · score 1.5

Go to course ↗

An advanced UC Berkeley course on deep neural networks covering their design, visualization, and how they work, including generative models such as those behind Stable Diffusion. Lecture videos are reportedly available only to Berkeley students.

Commenters describe it as really good for advanced deep learning and point to its assignments as a way to learn implementation, alongside Stanford CS 231N and MIT 6.S191.
