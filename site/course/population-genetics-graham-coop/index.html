Population Genetics (Graham Coop)

UC Davis

1 recommender · 1 thread · recommended in 1 year, 2021 · score 0.7

Search for it ↗

A university-level introduction to population genetics taught by Graham Coop at UC Davis, covering how genetic variation within and between populations is shaped by drift, selection, migration, and mutation. Its lectures are publicly available.

A commenter calls Coop's lectures the best real overview of current population genetics and says his teaching materials were inspirational for many people.
