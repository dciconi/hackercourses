A lecture series by Richard Schreier on delta-sigma modulators, the oversampling converter technique used in many analog-to-digital and digital-to-analog converters. It covers the theory and design of these modulators.

Commenters point to Richard Schreier's lectures as a deeper dive after introductory material on sigma-delta conversion.
