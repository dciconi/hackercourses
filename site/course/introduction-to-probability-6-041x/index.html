Introduction to Probability (6.041x)

edX

4 recommenders · 3 threads · recommended in 3 years, 2015–2018 · score 3.4

Go to course ↗

An introductory probability course from MIT taught by John Tsitsiklis, covering probabilistic models, conditioning, random variables, limit theorems, and Markov chains. It is offered on edX and mirrors MIT OpenCourseWare's 6.041 material.

Commenters call it amazing, very good, and one of the best courses they've taken, praising its rigor and the time-intensive exercises.
