COS 226 Algorithms and Data Structures

Princeton University

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2013–2023 · score 1.8

Go to course ↗

COS 226 is Princeton University's intermediate course on algorithms and data structures, covering fundamental data structures and algorithm design with an emphasis on practical implementation. The Coursera Algorithms I and II courses from Princeton cover about two-thirds of the same content with video lectures.

Commenters say it gives a good introduction to both algorithms and data structures and builds practical skills for leetcode-style interview questions, and one calls it one of their favorite classes with great lecture slides.
