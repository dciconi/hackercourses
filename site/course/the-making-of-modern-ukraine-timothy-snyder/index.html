The Making of Modern Ukraine (Timothy Snyder)

YouTube

1 recommender · 1 thread · recommended in 1 year, 2022 · score 1.0

A Yale lecture series by historian Timothy Snyder covering the history of Ukraine from the medieval period through the modern era, with particular attention to the 20th century, including the Holodomor and Soviet-era terror.

One commenter strongly recommends the series as a whole and singles out the lecture on Ukrainization, famine, and terror in the 1920s-1930s.
