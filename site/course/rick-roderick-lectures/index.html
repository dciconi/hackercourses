Rick Roderick lectures

rickroderick.org

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2017–2026 · score 1.8

A series of lectures by the late philosopher Rick Roderick on modern and postmodern European philosophy, including critical theory and the debates of the 1980s. A second series covers the history of Western philosophy.

Commenters call the lectures incredible and recommend them as a clear antidote to attacks on postmodernist thinkers.
