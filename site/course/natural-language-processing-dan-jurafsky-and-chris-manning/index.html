Natural Language Processing (Dan Jurafsky and Chris Manning)

YouTube

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2014–2017 · score 2.7

A Stanford course on natural language processing taught by Dan Jurafsky and Chris Manning, released as free YouTube lecture videos. It covers core NLP methods and is tied to Jurafsky and Manning's textbook Speech and Language Processing.

Commenters point to it as a strong free introduction to NLP from the authors of a well-regarded textbook, with one calling it a great online course and another strongly recommending it as a mini-course.
