An MIT Sloan course taught by Gary Gensler covering the fundamentals of money, cryptocurrencies, and blockchain technology, including Bitcoin and the economics and regulation of decentralized finance. Lectures are published free on MIT OpenCourseWare and YouTube.

Commenters call Gensler's lectures one of the best introductions to the fundamentals of money and crypto, and recommend them to anyone wanting to learn the basics.
