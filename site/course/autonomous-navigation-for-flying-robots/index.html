Autonomous Navigation for Flying Robots

edX

1 recommender · 1 thread · recommended in 1 year, 2016 · score 0.7

Go to course ↗

A TUM (Technical University of Munich) course on estimating a quadrotor's position from its sensor readings and navigating it along a trajectory. It covers state estimation and motion planning for flying robots.

One commenter calls it a good course from TUM's computer vision group, noting it was one of the few online offerings from that university.
