Deep Reinforcement Learning (CS 285)

UC Berkeley

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2020–2024 · score 2.2

Search for it ↗

CS 285 is UC Berkeley's graduate course on deep reinforcement learning, taught by Sergey Levine, covering policy gradients, value-based and actor-critic methods, and model-based RL. Its slides, lectures, and assignments are publicly available.

Commenters call it extremely insightful and highly recommend it, and point out that the slides, lectures, and assignments are all public.
