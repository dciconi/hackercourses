CS330 Deep Multi-Task and Meta Learning

Stanford University

3 recommenders · 4 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2020–2022 · score 2.3

Go to course ↗

CS330 is a Stanford graduate course on deep multi-task learning and meta-learning, taught by Chelsea Finn, covering how models learn to adapt quickly across many tasks. Its lectures are published on YouTube and its course site hosts the materials.

Commenters describe it as a one-stop resource for multi-task and meta-learning and list it among Stanford's excellent free AI course notes and lectures.
