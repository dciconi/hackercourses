Arithmetic, Population and Energy (Albert Bartlett)

YouTube

1 recommender · 1 thread · recommended in 1 year, 2020 · score 1.0

Search for it ↗

A lecture series by physicist Albert Bartlett on the mathematics of exponential growth, applying simple arithmetic to population growth and energy consumption. It is delivered as a long recorded talk available on YouTube.

Commenters describe it as absolutely amazing and say it changes how you see the news, though the only comment here mentions it briefly as a lecture video worth including.
