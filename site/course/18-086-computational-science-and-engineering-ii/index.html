18.086 Computational Science and Engineering II

MIT OpenCourseWare

1 recommender · 1 thread · recommended in 1 year, 2011 · score 0.7

Search for it ↗

A follow-on MIT OpenCourseWare course in computational science and engineering, covering numerical methods for scientific computing such as finite differences, finite elements, Fourier methods, and optimization. It is taught by Gilbert Strang and paired with his textbook Computational Science and Engineering.

One commenter wholeheartedly recommends it alongside Strang's book, saying it builds intuition for basic problems in scientific computing.
