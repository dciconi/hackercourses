Tiny Habits

BJ Fogg (Stanford Behavioral Design)

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2019–2020 · score 2.6

Search for it ↗

Tiny Habits is a free course by BJ Fogg of the Stanford Behavioral Design lab that teaches habit formation through small behaviors, anchoring them to existing routines, and celebrating small wins. It goes deeper into motivation, ability, and behavior design than introductory habit advice.

Commenters call it an incredible, free deep dive from the Master of this subject, and say it helped them build habits like flossing daily.
