A university-level introduction to database systems taught by Andy Pavlo at Carnegie Mellon, covering how databases store, index, and execute queries. It is delivered as a free YouTube lecture series.

Commenters describe it as a free CMU-quality course that explains the inner workings of databases, with one saying it helped them explain PostgreSQL internals to their boss.
