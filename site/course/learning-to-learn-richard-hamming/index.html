Learning to Learn (Richard Hamming)

YouTube

Hacker News: 1 recommender · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2021–2022 · score 1.1

Reddit: 2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2014–2018 · score 1.7

A 31-lecture video series by Richard Hamming, recorded late in his career at the Naval Postgraduate School, on how he approached learning and research across mathematics, computing, physics and technology, illustrated through his own life. Commenters note it also serves as a summary of his career.

Commenters call it an awesome 31-part course and describe it as a wonderful companion to Hamming's talk "You and Your Research".
