Natural Language Processing (Stanford)

Coursera

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2014–2019 · score 1.8

Search for it ↗

Stanford's introductory natural language processing course, taught by Chris Manning and Dan Jurafsky, covering core NLP techniques such as sequence models, Viterbi decoding, and CRFs. It was offered on Coursera as video lectures with homework assignments.

Commenters describe it as an excellent overview that serves as a good entry point into NLP papers and literature.
