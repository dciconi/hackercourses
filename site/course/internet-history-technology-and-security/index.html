Internet History, Technology, and Security

Coursera

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2018–2020 · score 1.5

Go to course ↗

A Coursera course by Chuck Severance covering the history of the internet from its beginnings to the present, including the technology and security aspects. It is described as light on networking detail such as TCP/IP, and includes archival footage and interviews with people who helped build the internet.

Commenters call it a great, lightweight introduction to internet history with interviews and archival footage, feeling more like an interactive documentary than a class.
