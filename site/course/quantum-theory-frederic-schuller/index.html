Quantum Theory (Frederic Schuller)

YouTube

1 recommender · 1 thread · recommended in 1 year, 2018 · score 0.7

Search for it ↗

A video lecture series by Frederic Schuller covering the mathematical foundations of quantum theory, including state spaces, linearity, and operators. It is presented as a rigorous, mathematics-oriented treatment of the subject.

Commenters recommend it alongside Brian Hall's book as a rigorous, mathematically clear treatment of quantum theory.
