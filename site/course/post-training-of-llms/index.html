Post-training of LLMs

DeepLearning.AI

A DeepLearning.AI course on the post-training stage of large language models, the work done after pre-training (instruction tuning, RLHF, alignment, tool use, and reasoning) that turns a next-token predictor into a usable model.

A commenter recommends it as a way to understand what happens after pre-training, namely the RLHF and alignment work that makes LLMs useful.
