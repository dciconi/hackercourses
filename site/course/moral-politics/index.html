Moral Politics is a Coursera course on how moral frameworks shape political views and debates. It draws on George Lakoff's work on framing, identity, and metaphor in politics.

Commenters point to it as a free route into moral philosophy as applied to politics, and one commenter links George Lakoff's moral politics lecture as a starting point.
