A Beginner's Guide to Irrational Behavior

Coursera

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2014–2020 · score 1.8

A Coursera MOOC by Dan Ariely introducing behavioral economics and the ways people make irrational decisions. It includes video lectures, guest talks, and a substantial set of required and recommended papers.

Commenters call it a favorite online course that was rigorous without being watered down, with curated papers they found especially valuable.

  1. Strongly recommendsDowwie2020in “Ask HN: What makes an online course amazing?”

    Dan Ariely's MOOC on Irrational Behavior (coursera) remains my favorite online course and one of the best overall.

    The content was engaging. It included guest talks. The course didn't shy from hard work, including dozens of required and recommended papers. The curated papers were my favorite part. The course exposed me to an entire ecosystem that I was unaware of. Too many MOOCs are watered down but this one was just right.

    My best advice is to create a course that management will resent open sourcing because it is so valuable. Demand autonomy and use it create a legacy.
