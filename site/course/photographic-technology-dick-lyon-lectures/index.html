Photographic Technology (Dick Lyon lectures)

Google

1 recommender · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2010–2011 · score 0.8

Search for it ↗

A series of roughly two dozen hours of lectures by Dick Lyon, given to Google engineers, covering the technology behind photography: optics, human color vision, silicon image sensors, and the algorithms that turn raw camera data into usable images.

Commenters recommend it as a deep, engineering-oriented treatment of the science behind digital photography, beyond typical photography instruction.
