Electric Charge, Field, and Potential

Khan Academy

1 recommender · 1 thread · recommended in 1 year, 2020 · score 1.0

A free Khan Academy physics unit introducing electric charge, electric force, electric fields, and voltage (electric potential). It is the first step in a sequence that continues to circuits and electrical engineering.

One commenter highly recommends it as the starting point for learning how electricity works, placed before the Circuits and Electrical engineering units.
