Melodeon building course

Emmanuel Pariselle

1 recommender · 1 thread · recommended in 1 year, 2018 · score 1.0

Search for it ↗

A residential melodeon building course run by Emmanuel Pariselle in France, in which participants assemble a melodeon and learn the processes, assembly jigs, and tuning work involved. Commenters describe it as more of an assembly course than a full build from scratch.

Commenters highly recommend it as a practical way to learn melodeon construction, with one describing it as very useful for understanding the processes and jigs.

I did it a few years ago in France when the pound was particularly strong, and had a lovely French holiday which just so happened to result in a new instrument at the end.
