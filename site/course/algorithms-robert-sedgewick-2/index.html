Algorithms is Robert Sedgewick's Stanford course, delivered as video lectures (a MOOC) covering the core algorithms and data structures taught alongside his textbook. It focuses on algorithm analysis, sorting, searching, graphs and related classic topics.

One commenter uses Sedgewick's Stanford MOOC videos as the refresher to reach for when they need to revisit a specific algorithm.
