Distributed Systems (Martin Kleppmann)

YouTube

3 recommenders · 3 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2023–2024 · score 3.2

Go to course ↗

A university lecture series on distributed systems by Martin Kleppmann, covering topics such as replication, consistency, fault tolerance, and consensus, with accompanying lecture notes. It is a concise course that complements his book Designing Data-Intensive Applications.

Commenters call it excellent and say Martin is a great teacher, with the lecture notes and videos praised as high quality and concise.
