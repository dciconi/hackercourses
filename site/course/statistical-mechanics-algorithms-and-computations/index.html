Statistical Mechanics: Algorithms and Computations

Coursera

2 recommenders · 3 threads · recommended in 3 years, 2016–2024 · score 2.0

Go to course ↗

A Coursera course on statistical mechanics that teaches the subject through computation, with Python programs for simulating physical systems and Markov chain Monte Carlo methods. It is taught by Daniel Schroeder-style physics lecturers at an intermediate university level, with a focus on building simulations step by step.

Commenters call it an excellent, highly recommended course with clear explanations and many Python programs, and one calls its first lecture a favorite introduction to Markov chain Monte Carlo.
