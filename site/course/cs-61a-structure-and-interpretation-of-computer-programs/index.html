CS 61A: Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs

UC Berkeley

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2017–2018 · score 1.5

Search for it ↗

CS 61A is UC Berkeley's introductory programming course, covering functional and object-oriented programming, recursion, interpreters, and related abstraction techniques, using the Python-based Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs approach. Archived course offerings are available online with lecture materials and homework.

Commenters point to it as a free, fully online Berkeley intro course with archived lectures and assignments that can be used for self-study.
