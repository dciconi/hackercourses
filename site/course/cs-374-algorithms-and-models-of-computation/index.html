CS 374 Algorithms and Models of Computation

University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign

CS 374 at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign is an undergraduate course on algorithms and models of computation, taught from Jeff Erickson's lecture notes that later became his book Algorithms. It treats computer science as a branch of mathematics.

Commenters call it one of their favorite classes and say it changed how they approach their work by framing computer science as mathematics.
