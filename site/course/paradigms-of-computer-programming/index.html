Paradigms of Computer Programming

edX

2 recommenders · 2 threads · recommended in 2 years, 2018–2020 · score 1.8

Go to course ↗

A free edX course by UCLouvain's Peter Van Roy covering the main programming paradigms, using the Mozart/Oz language to compare functional, declarative, and other styles. It follows the material of his book Concepts, Techniques, and Models of Computer Programming.

Commenters call it a lucid treatment of the functional paradigm and a companion to Van Roy's well-regarded book CTM.
