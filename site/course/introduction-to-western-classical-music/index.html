Coursera

An introductory online course on Western classical music history and listening, offered on Coursera. It covers the major periods and forms of the Western classical tradition for learners without prior musical background.

Commenters point to it as the place to start for anyone without musical background, and one calls the Yale Coursera course on this subject the most amazing Coursera course they ever took.
