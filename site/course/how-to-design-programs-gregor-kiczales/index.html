How to Design Programs (Gregor Kiczales)

Coursera

5 recommenders · 5 threads · recommended in 3 years, 2013–2022 · score 5.7

Go to course ↗

A Coursera course based on the textbook How to Design Programs (HtDP2), taught by Gregor Kiczales, that teaches a systematic design recipe for writing programs in Racket/Scheme. It focuses on thinking through program design step by step rather than coding by trial and error.

Commenters describe it as a rare course that teaches thinking and design before coding, and one that makes the HtDP method click.
