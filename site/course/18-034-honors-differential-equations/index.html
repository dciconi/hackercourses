18.034 Honors Differential Equations

MIT OpenCourseWare

1 recommender · 1 thread · recommended in 1 year, 2019 · score 0.7

MIT's honors version of introductory differential equations, offered through MIT OpenCourseWare, aimed at students who want more theory than the standard ODE course. It is presented as a more proof-oriented treatment of the subject.

One commenter suggests it to a self-studier as the more theoretical option compared with the standard differential equations course.
