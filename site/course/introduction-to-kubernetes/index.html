Introduction to Kubernetes

edX

An introductory edX course on Kubernetes, the container orchestration system, aimed at people new to the topic. It is offered by the Linux Foundation with CNCF support.

Commenters recommend it as a free starting point for someone fresh to Kubernetes, noting it gives a solid introduction and useful notes.
