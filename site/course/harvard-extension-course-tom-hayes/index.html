A Harvard Extension School course taught by Tom Hayes, the co-author of the student manual for The Art of Electronics, covering practical electronics circuit design and lab work. Commenters describe it as an in-person course offered through Harvard Extension in the Boston area.

One commenter says it was a course they loved that set them on the path to becoming an embedded engineer, and they suggest it to anyone in the Boston area.
