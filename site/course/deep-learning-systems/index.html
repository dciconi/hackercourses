Deep Learning Systems

Carnegie Mellon University

1 recommender · 1 thread · recommended in 1 year, 2023 · score 0.7

A Carnegie Mellon University course on the internals of deep learning systems, covering how deep learning libraries are built, including automatic differentiation and GPU-backed tensor computation. It goes deeper into library implementation than a typical introductory deep learning course.

A commenter calls CMU's open deep learning courses very good and says this one dives much deeper into the internals of the libraries.
