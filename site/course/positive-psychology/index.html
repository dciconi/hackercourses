Positive Psychology

Harvard University

1 recommender · 1 thread · recommended in 1 year, 2019 · score 1.0

Search for it ↗

Harvard's Positive Psychology course, taught by Tal Ben-Shahar, covers the scientific study of happiness, well-being, and what makes people flourish. It is an online course rather than a general psychology survey.

Commenters call it a great course with a great teacher and point to its lectures as a good entry into the field.
