Entrepreneurial Thought Leaders

Stanford University

1 recommender · 1 thread · recommended in 1 year, 2014 · score 0.8

A free Stanford lecture series, run through the Stanford Entrepreneurship Corner, in which founders and business leaders speak on startups, negotiation and building companies. Speakers have included Marc Andreessen, Aaron Levie, Reid Hoffman and Marissa Mayer.

One commenter calls a negotiation talk in the series one of the best lectures they've heard and says they've recommended it dozens of times.
