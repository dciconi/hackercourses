11-761 Language and Statistics I

Carnegie Mellon University

A Carnegie Mellon graduate course on statistical methods for natural language processing, covering the probabilistic and machine learning foundations needed to work with text. The commenter describes it as covering the prerequisite ML alongside text-focused material.

Commenters call it a great course for anyone who wants to work primarily with text, since it covers the prerequisite machine learning as well.
