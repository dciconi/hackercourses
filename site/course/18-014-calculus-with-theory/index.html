18.014 Calculus with Theory

MIT OpenCourseWare

1 recommender · 1 thread · recommended in 1 year, 2019 · score 0.7

A proof-oriented first-year calculus course from MIT OpenCourseWare, the theory-focused alternative to 18.01 covering single-variable calculus with rigorous justification of its concepts.

One commenter suggests it as the more theoretical option for a self-studier past the usual freshman sequence, noting it was not available when they were a student.
